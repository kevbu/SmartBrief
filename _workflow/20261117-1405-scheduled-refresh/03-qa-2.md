### Still open from run 1 (minor, out of fix scope)

- `tickRunning` and `lastFailureAt` are module-level in `src/lib/scheduler.ts`, while the start guard and the new piggyback slots are on `globalThis`. Review item 6, deliberately left out of Fix Round 1. No functional defect under `next start`.

### Observation on fix 1 (minor)

- A piggyback slot is released only when its task settles. If `pollImap()` or `detectAndPush()` ever hangs (never resolves), that task is skipped for the rest of the process lifetime. Before the fix a hang only blocked that one run. Severity: minor, not a regression in normal operation; a timeout would bound it.
