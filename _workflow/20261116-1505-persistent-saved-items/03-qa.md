## QA fixture

Copy of `prisma/dev.db` via `sqlite3 .backup` (384 articles, 20 current-batch stories), then:
- 1 recent article saved; 1 article saved and backdated to 2026-01-01 (title prefixed "QA ancient saved").
- 1 current-batch story saved; 1 extra saved story `qa-old-saved` with `createdAt` one day older than the batch.
